# Hardware & Firmware Exploitation

<div class="card bg-dark border-secondary mb-4">
  <div class="card-body">
    <h3 class="card-title text-light">Automotive CAN Bus Diagnostics</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>The Problem:</strong> Factory vehicle issues require proprietary, expensive diagnostic tooling to interface with the ECU.</li>
      <li><strong>The Hack:</strong> Interfaced directly with CAN bus telemetry via OBD integration and laptop-based terminals to intercept and decode vehicle diagnostic data.</li>
      <li><strong>Tech Stack:</strong> ECU Telemetry, CAN protocol, Serial Communication.</li>
    </ul>

  <div class="card-body">
    <h3 class="card-title text-light">Mechatronics & Power Conversion</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>The Problem:</strong> Prototyping high-draw physical components requires stable, variable power supplies often unavailable outside commercial labs.</li>
      <li><strong>The Hack:</strong> Reverse-engineered and converted an ATX PC power supply to drive heavy-duty 12V automotive tire inflators and custom electronics.</li>
      <li><strong>Tech Stack:</strong> Soldering, Circuit Teardowns, Structural Mechanics.</li>
    </ul>
