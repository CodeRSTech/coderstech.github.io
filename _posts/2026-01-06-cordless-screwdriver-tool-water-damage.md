<p class="text-white-50 mb-4">Complete teardown, solvent flush, and planetary gear rebuild after a 5-minute deep water submersion.</p>

**The Incident & Emergency Triage**
Keeping a cordless screwdriver where the laundry was proved to be a costly mistake. By the time I could figure out what was wrong, the said tool had already spent a few minutes deep in detergent water. I panicked and immediately disassembled the screwdriver, wiped it and put it in sealed in a bag with silica gel packets to draw out ambient moisture while I figure out what to do next.

<img src="/assets/images/posts/disassembly.png" class="img-fluid rounded mb-3" alt="Water damaged internals">

**Analysis**

In short, water had gotten everywhere.  There was water on the PCB, the battery and the motor was the most flooded. 

<img src="/assets/images/posts/battery_housing_and_pcb.png" class="img-fluid rounded mb-3" alt="PCB and Battery Housing">

**Cleanup**

Thankfully, WD-40 helped displace all the moisture inside the motor and the other parts, but that was a problem on its own, those who have used it well know that better. A drill was fastened to the motor shaft and operated at high speeds, letting centrifugal forces drive the liquids out. The motor was then rinsed with 99.9% IPA and spun again. Other components were also cleaned with WD-40 and IPA, which stripped away the oily WD-40 residue and lingering mineral deposits, leaving a dry, clean surface ready for new lubrication.

**Consequences of cleanup**

While WD-40 solved the water problem, it dissolved planetary gears' grease and things quickly escalated. Fortunately, plastic-safe lithium grease was available. All gears were cleaned and lubricated and, the motor housing was re-assembled.

<img src="/assets/images/posts/internal_gears.png" class="img-fluid rounded mb-3" alt="Internal Planetary Gears">

**The Final Rebuild**

To prevent torque shear, the plastic motor mounting plate was secured by carefully applying cyanoacrylate to ensure the screws bit into their original threads without stripping the housing. The tool is now fully operational with zero alignment play.
