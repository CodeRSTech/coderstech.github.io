**The Diagnostic**
The primary issue was severe corrosion on the stator coils. 

<img src="/assets/images/posts/battery_housing_and_pcb.png" class="img-fluid rounded mb-3" alt="">

**The Rebuild**
Rewound the coil, re-soldered the main contacts, and applied dielectric grease to prevent future water ingress.
