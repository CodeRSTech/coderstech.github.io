<p class="text-white-50 mb-4">Complete teardown, solvent flush, and planetary gear rebuild after a 5-minute deep water submersion.[cite: 2]</p>

<h3 id="triage" class="text-light mt-5">The Incident & Emergency Triage</h3>
<p>Storing the cordless screwdriver near the laundry resulted in a severe liquid intrusion event, with the tool spending several minutes submerged in detergent water[cite: 2]. Upon discovery, I immediately disassembled the screwdriver, wiped down the standing water, and sealed the internal components in a bag with silica gel packets to draw out ambient moisture while formulating a recovery plan[cite: 2].</p>

<img src="/assets/images/posts/disassembly.png" class="img-fluid rounded mb-4 mt-4" alt="Water damaged internals">

<h3 id="analysis" class="text-light mt-5">Analysis</h3>
<p>The water ingress was extensive[cite: 2]. A diagnostic teardown revealed water pooling across the PCB and battery housing, with the primary DC motor suffering the most severe flooding[cite: 2].</p>

<img src="/assets/images/posts/battery_housing_and_pcb.png" class="img-fluid rounded mb-4 mt-4" alt="PCB and Battery Housing">

<h3 id="cleanup" class="text-light mt-5">The Cleanup & Centrifugal Purge</h3>
<p>To displace the moisture inside the motor and surrounding components, I applied a generous coating of WD-40[cite: 2]. To mechanically extract the internal liquids, I fastened an external drill to the flooded motor's shaft and operated it at high speeds, utilizing centrifugal force to physically drive the water out[cite: 2]. The motor was subsequently flushed with 99.9% Isopropyl Alcohol (IPA) and spun again[cite: 2]. The remaining components were also treated with WD-40 and IPA to strip away oily residues and lingering mineral deposits from the detergent, resulting in a completely dry, clean surface[cite: 2].</p>

<h3 id="lubrication" class="text-light mt-5">Mechanical Re-Lubrication</h3>
<p>While the WD-40 successfully mitigated the water damage, its solvent properties completely dissolved the factory grease within the planetary gear assembly[cite: 2]. To resolve this, all gears were meticulously cleaned and re-lubricated using a plastic-safe lithium grease before reassembling the main motor housing[cite: 2].</p>

<img src="/assets/images/posts/internal_gears.png" class="img-fluid rounded mb-4 mt-4" alt="Internal Planetary Gears">

<h3 id="rebuild" class="text-light mt-5">The Final Rebuild</h3>
<p>To prevent torque shear during final assembly, I carefully applied cyanoacrylate to the plastic motor mounting plate[cite: 2]. This acted as a liquid thread-locker, ensuring the screws bit firmly into their original threads without stripping the housing[cite: 2]. The tool is now fully operational with zero alignment play[cite: 2].</p>
