<p class="text-white-50 mb-4">Diagnosis and bare-metal repair of a shorted armature.</p>

<img src="/assets/images/tool-before.jpg" class="img-fluid rounded mb-3" alt="Water damaged internals">

**The Diagnostic**
The primary issue was severe corrosion on the stator coils. 

**The Rebuild**
Rewound the coil, re-soldered the main contacts, and applied dielectric grease to prevent future water ingress.
